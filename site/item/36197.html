Action text

"I move that the FY 2025 delinquent real estate property tax list, as submitted by the Director of Finance, exclusive of those taxes paid since June 30, 2025, be published in a newspaper of general circulation in Town and posted on the Town’s website.  I further move that delinquent real estate property taxes be recorded as a tax lien against said properties.  And I further direct the Director of Finance to write off delinquent annual tax bills of $5 or less.”
