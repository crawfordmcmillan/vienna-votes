How Vienna votes.

Results from the Town of Vienna’s four precincts in federal, statewide, and town elections since 2013, from the Virginia Department of Elections’ official records.

How to read these numbers

The Town of Vienna is served by four Fairfax County precincts — Vienna #1, #2, #4, and #6. These are the same four precincts that carry the town’s own mayoral and council elections, but precinct lines are drawn by Fairfax County and may not match the town boundary exactly, so federal and state totals here are a close approximation of the town’s vote, not an official town tally.

Before roughly 2023, Virginia counted most absentee and early ballots in a countywide “central absentee precinct” that cannot be attributed to individual precincts. Where that applies, it is noted on the contest, with the countywide number — the precinct figures for those years reflect only ballots reported at the precinct level. Town contests (2023 onward) count town voters only.
