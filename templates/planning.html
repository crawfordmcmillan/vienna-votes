How to read this list

The Planning Commission reviews development plans and the comprehensive plan. The Board of Zoning Appeals decides variances and appeals. The Board of Architectural Review approves signs and building exteriors. The Windover Heights Board of Review covers the town’s historic district. The town’s system records what was on each agenda; the outcome of a case is in the meeting minutes, linked on each row where published.
